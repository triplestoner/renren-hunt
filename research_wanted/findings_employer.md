# Wanted (원티드) Employer Interaction Flow Research

## Executive Summary

Wanted (원티드) is a leading Korean recruitment platform with over 3 million users and 25,000+ companies. It offers a comprehensive employer service platform with AI-powered candidate matching, talent pool exploration, interview coordination, and recruitment analytics.

---

## 1. Company Registration and Verification

### Registration Process

**Step-by-step flow:**
1. Access company service page → Click [Company Signup] button
2. Complete member registration process
3. Complete company service registration procedure
4. Company approval completion → Sign usage agreement (electronic signature via ModuSign) / Register card + Upload images
5. Register job postings (by job category/role and position level)

### Key Features:
- **Electronic Contract Signing**: Uses ModuSign for electronic agreements
- **Business Registration Verification**: Business registration certificate required with matching information
- **Free Office Photography**: For Seoul/Pangyo area companies, Wanted provides free office photo service
- **Dual Contract Options**: 
  - Usage agreement (이용동의서) - electronic signing
  - Card registration (카드 계약) - direct payment setup
- **Multi-User Support**: No additional cost or personnel limit for multiple administrators/reviewers

### Approval Process:
- Business registration verification required
- Company approval takes business days
- If approval is held, notification email sent with reason
- Duplicate company detection handled with admin intervention

### Account Types:
- **Administrator (관리자)**: Full access to company settings, billing, member management
- **Reviewer (리뷰어)**: Limited to job posting management and candidate review

**Source:** https://help.wanted.co.kr/hc/ko/articles/8054210281241

## 2. Job Posting Creation and Management

### Posting Creation

**Required Fields:**
- **Job Title (공고명)**: Only job position name allowed; other info auto-removed during approval
- **Job Category/Role (직군/직무)**: Select up to 3 categories for accurate matching
- **Experience Level (경력)**: Separate postings for entry-level (0-1 years) vs experienced
- **Location (근무지)**: Address input shows map on posting
- **Expected Salary (예상 연봉)**: Used for statistics only, never publicly disclosed
- **Posting Date (게시일)**: Immediate or custom schedule
- **Deadline (마감일)**: Always open or custom date
- **Notification Email**: For applicant alerts (defaults to company info email if not specified)

### Posting Features:
- **Draft Save (임시저장)**: Save progress before publishing
- **Preview (미리보기)**: View final posting format before submission
- **Copy Posting**: Duplicate existing job postings
- **External Link Usage**: Generate job posting links for use on all sites immediately
- **Wanted Site Toggle**: Can turn off Wanted display while keeping link active

### Approval Timeline:
- Job posting review within **24 business hours** (9am-6pm)
- Content modifications require re-approval (24 hours)
- External link-only postings update immediately without approval
- Deadline/tech stack/email modifications don't require additional approval

### Posting Management:
- **Settings Button**: Manage deadline, tech stack, notification email
- **Edit Function**: Modify content with re-approval
- **Close Posting**: Manual closing available

**Source:** https://help.wanted.co.kr/hc/ko/articles/8054227285017

## 3. Candidate Screening and Evaluation Tools

### Talent Pool Exploration Service (인재풀 탐색)

**Overview:**
- Direct talent search without waiting for applications
- AI-powered candidate recommendations
- Interview proposal system

### Key Benefits:
- **Fast Hiring**: Average 3 days for interview acceptance
- **Reduced Hiring Time**: Average 15 days shorter
- **Cost Efficiency**: 65% of users experience cost reduction
- **No Additional Fees**: Regardless of number of hires

### AI Recommendation Features:
- **80万+ Hiring Data**: Based on extensive recruitment data analysis
- **High Accuracy Matching**: AI recommends candidates with high pass probability
- **1/6 Hiring Time**: Compared to traditional job posting approach

### Search Capabilities:
- Multiple search criteria for talent lookup
- Profile preview cards (school, previous company, core competencies)
- Bookmark/favorite candidates function

### Usage Process:
1. **View Talent Profiles**: Search with various keywords
2. **Resume Review & Interview Proposal**: 
   - Open detailed resume
   - Send customized interview proposal message
   - Include company info, position details, salary, benefits
3. **Interview on Acceptance**: 
   - Candidate name and contact revealed on acceptance
   - Direct contact for interview scheduling

### Pricing:
- **Unlimited Plan**: Unlimited profile views, unlimited duration
- **Fee Structure**: No upfront cost; 7% of hired candidate's annual salary (charged on successful hire)

**Source:** https://help.wanted.co.kr/hc/ko/articles/360035727312

## 4. Interview Coordination Features

### Interview Scheduling System

**Availability:**
- Available after document screening pass
- NOT available for "Received" or "Final Acceptance" status candidates

### Interview Request Process:

**1. Interview Permission Management (면접 권한 관리)**
- Select from authorized reviewers/admins for the job posting
- Only members with interview permission can view/modify schedules
- Tip: If reviewer not visible, add permission via "Job Posting Management → Settings"

**2. Date & Time (날짜 및 시간)**
- Add desired schedules via [+Add Date] button
- Maximum 5 schedule options can be proposed to candidate

**3. Location (장소)**
- Online or Offline interview options
- Online URL can be left blank if not yet confirmed
- Update URL later via interview modification

**4. Contact Information**
- Mandatory contact for interview coordination
- Phone or email (at least one required)

**5. Notice/Announcements (공지사항)**
- Add interview instructions for candidate reference

### Confirmation Flow:
- Interview confirmed on candidate's selected schedule
- Confirmation notification sent to authorized reviewers/admins
- Interview status visible in applicant card view

### Reminder System:
- **Automatic Reminder**: Sent 1 day before interview at 10am
- Sent to both authorized members and candidate

### Modification:
- Interview details can be modified after confirmation
- Change notification sent to candidate

**Source:** https://help.wanted.co.kr/hc/ko/articles/18881394938905

## 5. Recruitment Analytics and Data

### Platform Statistics (from Wanted materials):
- **User Base**: 3+ million users domestically and internationally
- **Company Usage**: 25,000+ companies
- **Hiring Success Rate**: 60% within 1 month of posting
- **Cost Reduction**: 40%+ compared to traditional channels
- **Lead Time Reduction**: Average 15 days shorter

### Applicant Management Features:
- **Daily Applicant Reports**: Sent to registered notification email (next day)
- **Status Tracking**: Document pass → Interview → Final acceptance/rejection
- **Hiring Stage Movement**: Update pass/reject status via applicant detail page
- **Automatic Notifications**: Results sent to applicants

### Candidate Evaluation:
- **Pass/Reject Processing**: Via [Recruitment Stage Movement] button
- **Stage History**: Track all recruitment process changes
- **Email Records**: View sent email history and content
- **Template Creation**: Create custom notification templates

### Fee Settlement:
- **Settlement Button**: Report hiring and process fee calculation
- **Fee Structure**: 7% of hired candidate's annual salary
- **Post-Hire Process**: Settlement after final acceptance

### Data Utilization:
- Salary info used only for statistics (never public)
- Recruitment process tracking per applicant
- Response rate tracking affects platform ranking
