## 3. Business Model & Revenue Sources

### Primary Revenue Streams

#### 1. Recruitment Platform Services
- **Pay-on-hire model**: Companies pay when successfully hiring matched candidates
- **Job posting services**: Premium job posting options
- **AI matching fees**: Fees for AI-powered candidate matching

#### 2. Subscription Services
- **Wanted X**: Premium membership for career development and community access
- **Wanted LaaS (LLM-as-a-Service)**: Generative AI development tool subscription for enterprises
- **Enterprise ATS**: Recruitment management system subscription

#### 3. AX (AI Transformation) Services
- AI literacy training for companies
- Gen.AI Training programs
- Promptathon events
- AI Agent Builder services
- AX consulting packages

#### 4. Education & Training (Preonboarding)
- Career development programs
- Job-specific bootcamps (AI, development, marketing, etc.)
- HR Bootcamp for HR professionals

#### 5. Global Services
- **Wanted Japan**: Local subsidiary for Japan market recruitment
- **HR Booster**: Japan local HR expert fulfillment services
- **Global Membership**: Bilingual talent matching

#### 6. Advertising Services
- Display advertising targeting 3.18 million users
- Company branding packages

#### 7. Wanted Lab Partners
- Strategic investment in HR/SaaS/AI/AX companies
- Service integration partnerships
- IPO and M&A advisory

### Pricing Strategy
- Shift to subscription model ("정액제") in recent quarters
- Reward services: Wanted Mileage (for companies), Wanted Points (for users)
- Various promotional packages for companies

## 4. Competitive Landscape in Korean Job Market

### Major Competitors

| Platform | Founded | Focus | Differentiation |
|----------|---------|-------|-----------------|
| **Saramin** | 2000 | General recruitment | Largest traditional platform, broad job categories |
| **JobKorea** | 2000 | General recruitment | Corporate focus, established player |
| **Wanted** | 2015 | Digital talent & AI | AI matching, startup focus, tech-oriented |

### Wanted's Competitive Advantages

1. **AI-First Approach**: 10 million matching data points powering AI agent for recruitment
2. **Digital Talent Focus**: Specialized in developers, designers, marketers (vs. general recruitment)
3. **Startup Ecosystem**: Strong ties with venture capital firms and startups
4. **Global Expansion**: Active Japan market presence with local subsidiary
5. **AX Services**: Unique AI transformation consulting offering
6. **Community Building**: High Five conferences, career communities

### Market Position
- Differentiated from traditional platforms (Saramin, JobKorea) by:
  - Focus on digital talent vs. general job seekers
  - AI matching technology vs. keyword search
  - Startup-friendly vs. large corporate focus
  - Subscription models vs. pay-per-posting

### Partnerships & Ecosystem
- Partnership with Naver Cloud for HyperClovaX-based LaaS
- Collaboration with government (Employment Ministry) for AI services in public platforms
- University partnerships (Kyunghee University, Sookmyung Women's University) for AI education
- Venture capital firm partnerships (Kakao Ventures, Mashup Ventures, Future Play)

## Key Takeaways

### Wanted's Unique Position
1. **AI-Powered Matching**: 10 million matching data trained AI agent for precise talent-company matching
2. **Career Super App Vision**: Expanding beyond recruitment to cover all career needs (education, community, global)
3. **AX Pioneer**: Leading AI transformation services for Korean enterprises
4. **Global Bridge**: Strong Japan market presence with local operations

### Business Model Evolution
- Shift from pure recruitment platform to multi-service HR tech ecosystem
- Subscription revenue growing alongside transaction-based recruitment fees
- AX consulting as new growth driver

### Competitive Strategy
- Differentiation through technology (AI) rather than scale
- Focus on underserved segments (digital talent, startups)
- Global expansion as growth lever when domestic market matures
